# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to be useful to as many people as possible, and that only works if
the people building it feel safe and respected. We — maintainers and contributors alike —
commit to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, caste, color, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We commit to acting in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy.

## What we value

Behavior that helps the community thrive includes:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences, even when you disagree.
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully — assume good intent, and aim to be helpful
  rather than to win.
- Taking responsibility when you get something wrong, apologizing to those affected, and
  learning from it.
- Optimizing for what's best for the whole community, not just for yourself.

## What is not acceptable

The following have no place here:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory remarks, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, whether in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information — such as a physical or email address —
  without their explicit permission.
- Posting others' (or your own) **real personal data** in issues, pull requests, or
  discussions. This project is designed so that real resumes, emails, or API keys are
  never needed to reproduce a problem — please redact them.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and are expected to respond to
unacceptable behavior fairly and promptly. They may edit, remove, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of
Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when it's appropriate to do so.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull
requests, and discussions — and also when someone is officially representing the project
elsewhere, such as posting from an official account or speaking as a project
representative at an event, online or off.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer
via GitHub ([@hrdevop](https://github.com/hrdevop)). All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. We will respect the privacy and safety of anyone who
reports an incident.

## How issues are handled

Maintainers will use the following guidelines to decide on consequences, scaling the
response to the severity and pattern of the behavior:

1. **A quiet word.** For a first or minor lapse — unprofessional or unwelcome language —
   you may receive a private note explaining what was wrong and why, possibly with a
   request to apologize. No public call-out.
2. **A warning.** For a clearer violation or a repeated one, a formal warning, with the
   expectation of no further contact with those involved (including moderators) for a set
   period. Continuing past this can lead to a ban.
3. **A temporary ban.** For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior, a
   temporary suspension from any interaction with the community. Breaking the terms of a
   temporary ban can lead to a permanent one.
4. **A permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, sustained harassment, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people, a permanent removal from the community.
